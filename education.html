Featured Coursework

SFS 216: The Sustainable Plate

A food course that surveys agriculture by sector, including livestock, grains, and fruits and vegetables. I learned how dietary choices affect health, the environment, and the people who produce and process food.

SOS 220: Systems Thinking

An introduction to systems thinking and complexity science, applied to sustainability issues. The course builds skills in reading critically, expressing ideas clearly, and seeing the many systems that shape our lives.

SOS 310: Equity, Justice and Sustainability

Explores the social justice side of sustainability science and practice. Sustainability problems span time, space, and institutions, and they threaten tightly linked social, economic, and ecological systems. We studied both theory and practice.

SOS 327: Sustainable Food and Farms

Covers food security, rural livelihoods, and food sovereignty, and examines the social, ecological, economic, and institutional challenges across the food system, from production and processing to distribution, consumption, and waste. I built a food system framework and used it to assess the sustainability of one part of the local food system.

GlobalResolve Lab (Saint Lucia project)

GlobalResolve brings together students, faculty, and global partners from fields such as engineering, media, medicine, and sustainability to work on human-centered design projects. It works directly with local NGOs, universities, schools, and community leaders rather than imposing outside fixes. I joined the Saint Lucia project, working with Hotel Chocolat and Helen's Daughters.

How My Education Connects to My Work

  • Research: My coursework in systems and food systems supports my role as Project Lead (Research Manager) for Forager at the ASU Luminosity Research Lab, where I work on sustainable harvesting and indigenous foodways.
  • Applied sustainability: At Hayden Flour Mills, I audited the retail space and recommended eco-friendlier packaging, composting, and recycling options.
  • Justice and community: My Justice Studies minor connects to my work with the ASU Climate Action Campaign and the GlobalResolve project, which centers local partners.
