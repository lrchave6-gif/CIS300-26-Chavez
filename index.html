About Me

Profile photo

Hi, I'm Lindsey Chavez

I am an undergraduate student at ASU in Barrett, The Honors College, pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Sustainable Food Systems with a minor in Justice Studies. I was born in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and have lived in Arizona for 13 years. I have been interested in sustainability since my freshman year of high school. Every step of my degree has shown me why it matters to tackle critical sustainability concerns in the food system, and how the lack of just practices has led to many of them.

Outside of that interest, I love music, movies, reading, exercise, and cooking, but most of all hanging out with my friends. Since coming to ASU, I have helped run three student organizations, joined a research lab on campus, and studied abroad in the Ecuadorian Amazon and Saint Lucia. I will complete a capstone and thesis by the end of my degree. After graduating, I plan to pursue graduate school, with interests in research, working for the USDA, field work, consulting, and nonprofit work.

I created this site for my Web Design and Development class to show what I have done beyond what fits on my resume, and to help me and anyone I meet keep track of my activities.

Career Goals and Personal Statement

Since my freshman year of high school, cooking and the environment have shaped what I care about, and studying Sustainable Food Systems lets me combine the two. My Justice Studies minor keeps equity at the center of that work. At ASU, I have built my experience through research, student leadership, and hands-on work in the food industry.

As Project Lead of Forager in the Luminosity Lab, I help build a database and interactive map that share indigenous foodways and encourage responsible foraging. I also contribute to a soil health monitoring system for the Arizona Agriculture Hub for Innovation. As a USDA NextGen intern at Hayden Flour Mills, I completed a sustainability audit and developed a new cookie using surplus purple barley.

After earning my bachelor's degree, I plan to attend graduate school and become a researcher or specialist who helps farmers and communities adopt sustainable farming practices and promote food security.
